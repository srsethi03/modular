01

Integrated Climate-Resilient Rural Systems Programme

ESG-Aligned CSR-Ready Multi-Year Pan-India

A multi-component programme designed to strengthen climate resilience and livelihood security across rural production systems.

Components
  • • Climate-smart agriculture and crop diversification
  • • Water harvesting and irrigation efficiency systems
  • • Solar-powered irrigation and energy integration
  • • Soil health and regenerative land practices
  • • Farmer institutional strengthening
  • • Digital monitoring and performance tracking
Outcomes
  • ✓ 5,000–10,000 farmers adopting climate-resilient practices
  • ✓ Improved water availability and irrigation efficiency
  • ✓ Enhanced farm productivity and income diversification
  • ✓ Reduced climate risk exposure
  • ✓ Measurable ESG-aligned indicators
Geography: Climate-vulnerable districts across India.
