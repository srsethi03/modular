Our Work

Six integrated thematic pillars driving climate resilience, regeneration and sustainable livelihoods across India.

Thematic Pillars

Six Integrated Areas of Work

Each pillar is designed to integrate with the others, forming a unified climate-resilient and regenerative development system.

01

Climate Adaptation & Resilient Livelihoods

Climate variability is directly impacting agricultural productivity, rural incomes and community stability. CRDF designs and implements integrated climate adaptation systems for small and marginal farmers.

Interventions
  • Climate-resilient cropping systems
  • Crop diversification & risk reduction
  • Drought and flood adaptation
  • Climate advisory support
  • Farmer capacity building
Outcomes
  • ✓ Reduced climate vulnerability
  • ✓ Stabilised farm incomes
  • ✓ Improved agricultural resilience
02

Water Security & Resource Systems

Water stress is one of the most critical climate-related challenges in India. CRDF implements watershed-scale water security interventions to strengthen local resource availability.

Interventions
  • Micro-watershed development
  • Rainwater harvesting structures
  • Groundwater recharge systems
  • Efficient irrigation practices
  • Community water governance
Outcomes
  • ✓ Improved water availability
  • ✓ Increased irrigation efficiency
  • ✓ Strengthened water stewardship
03

Renewable Energy Integration

Energy transition is central to climate-resilient development. CRDF integrates renewable energy solutions into rural production systems.

Interventions
  • Solar irrigation systems
  • Decentralised renewable energy
  • Energy-efficient farm systems
  • Climate-smart energy integration
Outcomes
  • ✓ Reduced carbon intensity
  • ✓ Lower energy costs
  • ✓ Enhanced rural sustainability
04

Regenerative Landscapes & Soil Health

Healthy soils and ecosystems are foundational to sustainable development. CRDF promotes regenerative land management across degraded landscapes.

Interventions
  • Soil organic carbon enhancement
  • Agroforestry & tree systems
  • Integrated nutrient management
  • Biodiversity-supportive practices
Outcomes
  • ✓ Improved soil health
  • ✓ Enhanced ecosystem resilience
  • ✓ Long-term regenerative systems
05

Carbon & Nature-Based Solutions

As climate mitigation efforts intensify, there is growing need for carbon-ready frameworks. CRDF builds carbon readiness and nature-based solutions at scale.

Interventions
  • Carbon readiness assessments
  • Emission-reduction pathways
  • Nature-based interventions
  • Community carbon aggregation
Outcomes
  • ✓ Measurable emission reductions
  • ✓ Climate commitment alignment
  • ✓ Carbon-linked preparedness
06

Digital & AI-Enabled Climate Systems

CRDF integrates digital tools and data-driven monitoring to enhance transparency and scalability across all programme areas.

Interventions
  • Digital farmer databases
  • GIS-based planning & mapping
  • AI-enabled advisory tools
  • ESG-aligned dashboards
  • Performance tracking systems
Outcomes
  • ✓ Transparent monitoring
  • ✓ Data-driven decisions
  • ✓ Scalable implementation
