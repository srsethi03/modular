Who We Are

CRDF is a Section 8 not-for-profit organisation working across India to design, implement, and scale integrated climate-smart and regenerative rural development systems.

We operate at the intersection of climate action, water management, renewable energy, carbon sequestration, regenerative agriculture, and data-driven monitoring — creating measurable outcomes in resilience, livelihoods, and sustainability.
