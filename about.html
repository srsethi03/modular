Who We Are

A Systems-Based Climate Implementation Institution

Climate & Regenerative Development Foundation (CRDF) is a pan-India Section 8 not-for-profit institution established to design and implement integrated climate-resilient and regenerative development systems.

CRDF operates at the intersection of climate adaptation, water security, renewable energy, regenerative land management, carbon readiness and digital monitoring.

Our work aligns with evolving ESG frameworks, climate commitments and sustainable development priorities.

Why We Exist

Bridging Climate Ambition and Field-Level Action

India and many climate-vulnerable regions globally are entering a period of increasing climate volatility, water stress, soil degradation and livelihood vulnerability.

CRDF was established to bridge the gap between strategic sustainability commitments and scalable, integrated field-level implementation.

Vision & Mission

What We Stand For

Our Vision

A climate-resilient, regenerative and sustainable India

Where rural communities, landscapes and production systems are resilient to climate change, and contribute to sustainable development and global climate goals.

Our Mission

Design, implement and scale integrated development systems

Integrating climate resilience, water security, renewable energy, regenerative land management, carbon readiness and digital monitoring to create measurable and lasting impact.

Advisory Board

Strategic Advisors Guiding Our Vision

Dr. Hrusikesh Patro

Dr. Hrusikesh Patro is a distinguished agricultural scientist, academic leader, and institution builder with over 35 years of experience in agricultural education, research, extension, and university administration. He currently serves as Dean, Faculty of Agriculture at NIST University and previously held the position of Dean, College of Agriculture, OUAT. Throughout his career, he has provided strategic leadership in research management, institutional development, and academic governance while fostering collaborations with leading national and international organizations, including IRRI, ICRISAT, CABI, and Mississippi State University, USA. A Fulbright–Nehru Visiting Lecturer Fellow, Dr. Patro has authored over 130 publications and mentored numerous postgraduate and doctoral scholars. As a Member of CRDF's Advisory Council, he provides strategic guidance on climate-resilient agriculture, regenerative farming systems, research, innovation, and sustainable rural development, strengthening CRDF's vision of building climate-resilient and regenerative communities.

Mr. Anup Kumar Das

Mr. Anup Kumar Das is a distinguished Senior Meteorologist with the India Meteorological Department (IMD), Government of India, with extensive expertise in weather forecasting, climate science, agrometeorology, and disaster risk reduction. Throughout his career, he has played a key role in strengthening weather and climate services that support agriculture, water resource management, disaster preparedness, and climate resilience. His professional expertise spans operational meteorology, climate variability, early warning systems, hydrometeorological services, and weather-based decision support for sustainable development. As a Member of CRDF's Advisory Council, Mr. Das provides strategic guidance on climate risk assessment, weather intelligence, climate adaptation planning, and climate-resilient agriculture. His scientific expertise and practical insights strengthen CRDF's mission of integrating weather and climate information into regenerative agriculture, sustainable natural resource management, disaster resilience, and inclusive rural development.

Mr. Jitender Ghangas

Mr. Jitender Ghangas is a seasoned CSR and Sustainability leader with over 20 years of experience in designing, implementing, and scaling high-impact social development and corporate sustainability initiatives across India. He recently served as Associate Director – CSR & Sustainability at LTIMindtree, where he played a key role in advancing strategic partnerships, ESG initiatives, and inclusive development programmes. Throughout his career, he has led CSR and sustainability initiatives for leading organizations, including LTIMindtree, Ambuja Cements, Honda Power Products India, Reliance Cement, Dalmia Bharat Limited, and Amdocs India. His expertise spans CSR strategy, ESG, climate action, renewable energy, rural livelihoods, women entrepreneurship, and programme management. As a Member of CRDF's Advisory Council, Mr. Ghangas provides strategic guidance on CSR partnerships, sustainability, resource mobilization, and climate action, strengthening CRDF's mission of advancing regenerative development, climate resilience, and inclusive rural transformation.

Mr. Somnath Sahoo

Mr. Somnath Sahoo is a highly experienced watershed and natural resource management professional with over 45 years of expertise in watershed development, soil and water conservation, climate-resilient agriculture, and sustainable rural development. During his distinguished career with the Government of Odisha, he served in several key positions before retiring as Project Director, Watershed, Balangir. Following his retirement, he continued contributing to climate-smart agriculture as District Coordinator for a World Agroforestry Centre (ICRAF) research project, promoting biochar technologies and sustainable soil management among farming communities. His expertise spans watershed planning, land resource management, rainwater harvesting, soil health restoration, biochar applications, and community-based natural resource management. As a Member of CRDF's Advisory Council, Mr. Sahoo provides strategic guidance on regenerative agriculture, watershed management, climate adaptation, and sustainable livelihoods, strengthening CRDF's mission of building resilient landscapes and empowering rural communities through science-based and community-driven development.

Mr. Smruti Ranjan SethiRole Placeholder

Mr. Smruti Ranjan Sethi is a development strategist and climate resilience professional with over 20 years of experience in sustainable agriculture, climate adaptation, regenerative development, rural livelihoods, carbon markets, institutional strengthening and corporates. His expertise spans climate-smart agriculture, regenerative landscapes, carbon and nature-based solutions, water security, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), ESG partnerships, and programme design, positioning CRDF as a trusted catalyst for sustainable and inclusive rural transformation.

Mr. Bijay Kumar Satpathy Role Placeholder

Mr. Bijay Kumar Satpathy is a seasoned rural development and institutional management professional with over 20 years of leadership experience in sustainable agriculture, watershed development, farmer institution building, and livelihood promotion. His strategic vision, collaborative leadership, and commitment to farmer-centric development strengthen CRDF's mission of building climate-resilient, regenerative, and inclusive rural communities.
